Section 01 · Foundation

What is CPI?

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures the change over time in the retail prices of goods and services consumed by households. It is the official measure of retail inflation in India.

Why it matters

CPI is the reference used to understand inflation, how purchasing power changes, and the overall economic conditions households are facing.

How India Calculates CPI

A five-step methodology turns collected retail prices into a single national inflation signal.

Collect prices Retail and online price collection at regular intervals
4
Calculate item indices Price relatives for each item, referenced to the base year
